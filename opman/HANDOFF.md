## What this is
A study app for **VŠE 3BE216 Operations Management** (Felipe Martinez), built for Margo from her slides, the practice tests with answer keys, her team projects (iPhone 11 video deck, Team O Industry 4.0 sheet) and Wispr lecture recordings (context only). Materials: `~/Downloads/op.man/` (local only).

## Course facts (from the recordings)
- Tests on Moodle: open book, 90 minutes once started, everyone gets the same questions with different numbers. Answer format matters (percent vs decimal; decimal point vs comma).
- Test 1: resource standards, process standards, Little's Law, book (The Goal), theory. Test 2: operations planning (rules, Gantt, gradual/parallel/DBR), process design and choosing the technology, modern methods, Industry 4.0.
- Some theory also comes back in the state exam.
