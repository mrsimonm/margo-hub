JIT
Just-in-time offers a smooth, continuous and optimised
workflow, with carefully planned and measured work-cycle times
and on-demand movement of goods, reduces the cost of wasted
time, materials and capacity. Team members can concentrate on
their tasks without interruption, which leads to better quality,
timely delivery, and peace-of-mind for Toyota’s customers.
http://www.toyota-forklifts.co.uk/EN/company/Toyota-Production-System/Just-in-time/Pages/default.aspx
