1 1 · I M P R O V E M E N T P L A N
Three actions that improve flow without redesigning the whole factory
Each recommendation targets a different constraint: variability, defect escape and recovery speed.
01
Protect the test station
Use a small FIFO before testing, monitor queue
length and add parallel fixtures when utilisation
stays high.
FLOW
02
Catch defects earlier
Strengthen incoming/module checks and use
poka-yoke connectors and fixtures before the
phone reaches final test.
QUALITY
03
Make rework fast and
visible
Standardise the rework decision, record failure
codes and replace modules using a defined
escalation path.
RECOVERY
Trade-off to manage: more buffers and duplicate fixtures increase cost, so capacity should be added only where queue data proves the constraint.
