5. Scheduling
Steve Smith is a web site designer in a business school. Today he has returned from his annual
vacation (he finished all outstanding jobs before he left). Five design jobs are given to him
upon arrival at work. He gives the works codes from I to V based on the arrival of the works.
Steve has to decide in which sequence to undertake the jobs. He wants both to minimize the
average time the jobs are tied up in his office and, if possible, to meet the deadlines (delivery
times) allocated to each job.
However, he knows that he will be late in some jobs and early in other jobs. So, he wants to
apologise now for the late works in order to get some extra time. You have to identify for how
many days he will be late for each job and determine the date on which the job will be finished.
The working hours are usually from 9am to 5pm. Moreover, he is used to get 1 hour lunch at
12:15 and one 15min coffee break in the morning and a second one in the afternoon.
