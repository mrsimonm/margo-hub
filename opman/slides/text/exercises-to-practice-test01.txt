First Test
1. Resources standards
A construction company has an order to build 5 garages. The structure needs 3 steel profiles (IPE
140) with a length of 5.4 m and one of a length of 1.1 m. These steel profiles are sold in bars with a
length of 6 meters or 12 meters. The specific weight per linear meter is 12.9 kg. How many steel
profiles (IPE 140) of each length are needed to be ordered?
a) b) c) d) e) Calculate the total demand of steel profiles in meters and the coefficient of material
utilization for both profile lengths (CMU). The Cold saw has a width of 0.5 cm, and the heat
burns 0.5 mm of material on both sides of the cut.
Decide the number of long steel profiles to order and the number of short steel profiles to
order.
Next, consider whether buying steel profiles on the exact length required dimensions would
be beneficial. The supplier offers this option at 5% extra of the purchase price.
You have the opportunity to sell the non-required material at 50% of the market price
(19,50EUR/meter). Determine the sales price in CZK (1EUR = 25CZK)
The available truck has 10mts trunk capacity. You have to wait 2 days for the truck with
12mts trunk capacity. The extra cost of stopping manufacture is 5% of the material cost per
day. What truck will you use? Why?
2. Work standards
A batch (120 pieces) must be completed at the end of this Friday shift. Decide when this order must
start its manufacture process. Please specify the day and the number of the shift. (Monday = 1st shift.
One shift per day. 1 shift = 8 hours). Each shift uses 12min for general cleaning of the shop floor at
the end.
Calculate for LTL, µ and UTL:
a) Unit, batch, shift time.
b) c) Time to manufacture one batch
Determine the shift/day to order production

3. Resources standards
A construction company has an order to build one garage. The structure needs 4 steel profiles with a
length of 5.6 m. These steel profiles are sold in bars with a length of 12 meters. The supplier also
offers a length of 7 m, because these are leftovers from another job.
a) b) c) d) Calculate the total demand of steel profiles in meters and the coefficient of material
utilization for both profile lengths 7 meters and 12 meters. (Do not include the material
losses due Cold saw. The material tolerance covers this waste).
Decide on the length of the steel profiles. Argue your answer.
Next, consider whether it would be beneficial to buy steel profiles on the exact length
required dimensions. The supplier offers this option at 12% extra of the purchase price.
Write any further information you need in order to know argue your decision.
(SVOBODOVÁ, et al., 2012)
4. Little’s Law
a) A queue at the local coffee shop has 10 persons. One Barista is able to process orders around
5 minutes/per customer. Calculate how many baristas is needed to serve these 10 customers
in 25 min. (FACTORY PHYSICS, 2014)
b) A product is scheduled to wait for 2 days in finished goods inventory before shipping to the
customer. Use Little’s law to determine the total amount of inventory in finished goods.
Manufacture facilities provide 10 finished goods per day. (FACTORY PHYSICS, 2014)
c) A system at peak processes 8000 Transactions/Hour with an average response time of ~5s
per transaction. The average Think Time per user is ~30s. What is the number of concurrent
users on the system? (Practical Performance Analyst, 2014)
