Job description & Profile
• Bachelor's degree in a relevant topic area such as Computer Science,
Electronics or Software Engineering.
• Proven track record of commercial experience and managing project
deadlines
• Experience working with low power wireless communications such as
Bluetooth or WiFi.
• Knowledge of audio codecs such as I2C, SPI or R232, or audio
streaming.
• Experience using Software Development Kits (SDK) or Accessory
Development Kits (ADK).
• Good command of the English language - both written and oral
communication.
• Knowledge of software architecture.
• Experience in Embedded C or C programming.
