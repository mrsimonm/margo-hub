=== SLIDE 28 ===
Sequencing – In what order do things?
Steve Smith is a web site designer in a business school.
Returning from his annual vacation (he finished all
outstanding jobs before he left), five design jobs are given
to him upon arrival at work. He gives them the codes A to
E. Steve has to decide in which sequence to undertake the
jobs. He wants both to minimize the average time the jobs
are tied up in his office and, if possible, to meet the
deadlines (delivery times) allocated to each job.
