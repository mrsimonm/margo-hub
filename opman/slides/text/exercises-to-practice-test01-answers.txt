A construction company has an order to build 5 garages. The structure needs 3 steel profiles (IPE
140) with a length of 5.4 m and one of a length of 1.1 m. These steel profiles are sold in bars with a
length of 6 meters or 12 meters. Specific weight per a linear meter is 12.9 kg. How many steel
profiles (IPE 140) of each length are needed to be ordered?
a) Calculate the total demand of steel profiles in meters and the coefficient of material
utilization for both profile lengths (CMU). The Cold saw has a width of 0.5 cm, and the heat
burns 0.5 mm of material on both sides of the cut.
• My recommendation here is to focus in ONE bar of the longest possible purchase.
Calculate CMU there.
• Then, check how many long-required bars fit in one longest available (sold) bar. This
is, use the longest available bar as much as possible.
• When the long-required bars are been achieved, use the rest of the long available
bar to fit there the short-required bar.
• And the end, use small available bars to complete the demand of short-required
bars.

CMU is 96.11%, which mathematically is the highest that you can get. Thus, the answer for this part

Please note that the actual time structure of the calendar has not been considered and it has been
assumed that the activities can be carried out in parts between days. That is, this calculation works
on the assumption that the time of each activity can be divided. That is, if an activity has 25min (I

today, but, instead, I have to split the activity. So, the answer presented in this calculation has the
assumption that it is possible to work today only 20min on the activity and then the next day finish
the activity using the other 5min, to complete the 25 minutes of the activity.

necessary to check directly the calendar and the actual clock hours in order to determine the start
time of the order.

c) A system at peak processes 8000 Transactions/Hour with an average response time of ~5s
per transaction. The average Think Time per user is ~30s. What is the number of concurrent
users on the system? (Practical Performance Analyst, 2014)
• Throughput or X = 8000/3600 = 2.22 TPS
• We know: N = [Rt + Zt] * X ……….. [ N = Number of Users in the System, Rt = Response Time,
Zt = Think Time, X = Throughput ]
• Applying the equation: N = [ Rt + Z ] * X = [ 5 + 30 ] * 2.22 = 77.7
• The above system has approximately 77 users at peak driving 8000 Transactions/Hour.
