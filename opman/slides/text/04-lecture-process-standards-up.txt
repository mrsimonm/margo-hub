Maynard, 1940
Basic categories of motions
• 1) GET (G)
• 2) PUT (P)
• 3) APPLY PRESSURE (A) = 14 TMU
• 4) REGRASP (R) = 6 TMU
• 5) EYE ACTION (E) = 7 TMU
• 6) CRANK (C) = 15 TMUs/rev
• 7) STEPS (S) = 18 TMU/step
• 8) FOOT MOTION (F) = 9 TMU
• 9) BEND & ARISE (B) = 61 TMU

Capacity
Capacity is the maximum number of
products (outputs) that a operation (line,
workshop, office, etc.) produced in a
specific time period (year, month, shift)
under the specific conditions
(technological, organisational).
© Felipe Martinez

Process Capability

process. The simplest measure of capability (Cp) is given by the ratio of the
