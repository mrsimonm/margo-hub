- Production functions in the short run: TP, AP, MP
- Cost functions in the short run: TC, VC, FC and MC, AC, AVC, AFC
- Cost minimization optimum (isocost, isoquant)
- Cost functions in the long run: TC and MC, AC
- Revenue
