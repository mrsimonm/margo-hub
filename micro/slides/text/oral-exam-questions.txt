
=== SLIDE 1 ===
3BE123 - Microeconomics I Oral Examination Topic List - WT 2026/27
1. Consumer Optimum and Demand Curve under the Assumption of Direct Utility
Measurement
Total Utility. Marginal Utility. Consumer Optimum. Derivation of Demand Curve on the Basis of Cardinal
Approach.
2. Consumer Optimum and Demand Curve under the Assumption of Unmeasurable Utility
Indifference Curve Analysis. Indifference Curve. Budget Line. Consumer Optimum. Derivation of
Demand Curve on the Basis of Ordinal Approach.
3. Market and Market Elements
Definition of Demand. Movement along the Demand Curve and Shift of the Demand Curve. Definition of supply.
Movement along the Supply Curve and Shift of the Supply Curve. Price.
Market Equilibrium, Non-equilibrium situations (surplus/shortage).
4. Price Elasticity of Demand
Point Price Elasticity of Demand. Interval Price Elasticity of Demand (Midpoint Method). Relationship between
Price Elasticity of Demand, Marginal Revenue, and Total Revenue.
5. Short-run Production Function
Characteristics of the short run. Short-run Production Function. Total, Marginal, and Average Product.
Determination and standard shapes.
6. Short-run Cost Function
Characteristics of the short run. Firm's Short-run Costs. Derivation of Cost Function from Production Function.
Types, Standard Shapes, and Properties (determination) of Short-run Costs.
7. Long-run Production and Cost Functions
Characteristics of the Long Run. Isoquant, Isoquant Map. Isocost. Cost Minimization Optimum. Derivation of
Cost Function from Production Function. Types, Shapes, and Properties of Long-run Costs.
8. Revenue, profit and optimum of a firm under perfect competition
Total, Average, and Marginal Revenue in Perfect Competition. Explicit and Implicit Costs. Sunk Costs.
Accounting and Economic Profit. Optimal Level of Firm's Production - Golden Rule of Profit Maximization.
9. Revenue, profit and optimum of a firm under imperfect competition
Total, Average, and Marginal Revenue in Imperfect Competition. Explicit and Implicit Costs.
Accounting and Economic Profit. Optimal Level of Firm's Production - Golden Rule of Profit
Maximization.
10. Behavior of a perfectly competitive firm
Characteristics of perfect competition. Firm's Supply in the short run. The shutdown point. The break-even
point. Price elasticity of supply. Market supply. Efficiency of perfect competition.
11. Market equilibrium of perfect competition
Market Equilibrium and its Conditions. Equilibrium and Efficiency. Benefits of Exchange. Equilibrium with
Respect to Period Length. Equilibrium and Simultaneous Change of Supply and Demand.
12. General characteristics of imperfect competition
Reasons behind Existence of Imperfect Competition. Common and Distinctive Features of Perfect and Imperfect
Competition.
=== SLIDE 2 ===
13. Specific Characteristics of Imperfect Competition
Forms of Imperfect Competition. Monopoly. Oligopoly (Collusive Oligopoly, Oligopoly with A Dominant Firm).
Monopolistic Competition - Differentiation between Short-run and Long-run Situation.
14. Alternative Goals of a Firm
Types of Firms in a Market Economy. Alternative Goals of a Firm. Comparison of Behavior of a Profit
Maximizing Firm and a Revenue Maximizing Firm.
15. General Overview of Factor Markets
Essence and Specifics of Factor Markets. Types of factor prices. Composition of Factor Earnings (Transfer
Earnings and Economic Rent). Factor Demand. Factor Supply. Firm's Equilibrium in a Perfectly Competitive
Factor Market.
16. Labor Market
Labor as a Factor of Production. Individual Firm's Labor Demand. Labor Supply to an Individual Firm and Labor
Supply of a Single Worker. (Market) Equilibrium in a Perfectly Competitive Labor Market. Imperfections of a
Labor Market (Reasons and Consequences of Imperfections, Presence of Labor Unions and Monopsony).
17. Capital Market. Land Market.
Basic Terms of Capital Theory (Definitions and Classifications of Capital). Demand in a Capital Market. Supply
in a Capital Market. Equilibrium in a Capital Market. Market of Capital Goods. Specifics of a Land Market.
Economic Rent.
18. Market Failure and Governmental Influence
Forms of Market Failures. Monopoly Power. Externalities in Production. Externalities in Consumption. Positive
Externalities and Inefficiency. Negative Externalities and Inefficiency. Public Goods. Characteristics of Public
Goods. Optimal Amount of Public Goods. Asymmetric Information. Moral Hazard. Adverse Selection.
Governmental Influence on Microeconomic Agents.
19. Comparison of Efficiency of Different Market Structures – Productive Efficiency
Definition of Productive Efficiency. Productive Efficiency (Inefficiency) under Perfect Competition, Monopolistic
Competition, Oligopoly, and Monopoly.
20. Comparison of Efficiency of Different Marker Structures – Allocative Efficiency
Definition of Allocative Efficiency. Consumers Surplus. Producers Surplus. Allocative Efficiency (Inefficiency)
under Perfect Competition, Monopolistic Competition, Oligopoly, and Monopoly.
