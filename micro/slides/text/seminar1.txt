Starting Points
• Economics- the study of the allocation of scarce
resources among alternative uses.
• Microeconomics-the study of the economic choices
individuals and firms make and of how these choices
create markets.
• Economics is a human science but it uses math and
many simplifications and economic models to explain
economic reality.
• Models are simple theoretical descriptions that capture
the essentials of how the economy works.

=== SLIDE 24 ===
24
Short Run vs. Long Run
• Long run: A period of time long enough for a
firm to be able to vary all its factors of
productions or for a firm or household to adapt
fully to a price change.
• Short run: A period of time during which some
of a firm’s inputs cannot be varied or in which a
firm or household has not fully adapted to a
price change.
=== SLIDE 25 ===
25
Opportunity Cost
• Definition: The value of the next-best
alternative that must be forgone in order to
undertake an activity.
• The opportunity cost of any good or
services is the amount of other goods or
services that must be given up to obtain it.
