Principles of a Firm´
s Decisions About
Volume of Production and Price – rep.
Basic rule: Maximization of profit vs. Minimization of costs
Revenue (total revenue, TR) – how much we get from selling our
product? TR = P x Q
• Average revenue AR = TR / Q = (P.Q) / Q = P
• Marginal revenue MR = ΔTR / ΔQ
Costs (total costs, TC): Explicit (accounting) costs – actually spent
money + Implicit (opportunity costs)
Profit – economic profit vs. accounting profit:
Economic profit (EP) = accounting profit (AP) - opportunity costs
(OC, implicit costs)
EP (π) = AP – OC; AP = revenues (TR) – explicit costs

Characteristics of the Perfect Competition
• abstraction (predominantly model form)
• the product is homogeneous (identical)
• similar to the market of unprocessed agricultural products
• there are many small firms and many buyers
• no economic operator can determine the price
• sellers are price-takers (receive the market price); perfectly elastic
demand for the production of a firm
• sellers act independently of each other
• firms can leave and enter the industry freely
• sellers and buyers are well informed
• transaction costs are low

Perfectly Competitive Firm (short run)
• Profit maximizing quantity and price?
• Shut-down point – a firm may continue producing in the short run if
the revenue covers at least the variable cost and the loss might
reach the amount of the total fixed cost.
• The firm should shut dowvn (close down) if its total revenue is below
the total variable cost.
• Shut-down point by the economists is called the point in the graph
where the curve of the marginal revenue becomes the tangent line
of the curve of the average variable cost.
