Example 5 - Solution
The isocost intersects the x-axis for L = 40 and the y-axis
for K = 30.
a) If the price of labor is 150, then the total cost is 6,000
and the price of K is 200 .
b) In the cost optimum, the firm hires 20 units of labor,
then the cost of K is 3,000. c) In the cost optimum, the
marginal rate of technical substitution (MRTS) is
....150/200 = 0,75 .
6,000 – (150 . 20) = 6,000 – 3,000 = 3,000

Cost Minimization
• It solves the problem of minimizing the cost (TC) for
achieving the given volume of output Q
• Or maximizing production volume Q for a given cost
(TC)
Last-dollar rule: cost is minimized if inputs are chosen
so that the last dollar spent on labor adds as much
extra output as the last dollar spend on capital
