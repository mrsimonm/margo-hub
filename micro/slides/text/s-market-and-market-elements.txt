Categories of Demand Elasticity
￼
￼
￼
• │EPD│= 1 Unitary elasticity; the percentage change in quantity
demanded equals the percentage change in price; Total Revenue
(TR) does‘t change
• │EPD│ < 1 Demand is inelastic; the percentage change in
quantity demanded is less than the percentage change
in price; TR decreases
• │EPD│ > 1 Demand is elastic, the percentage change in quantity
demanded changes by greater percentage than does
the price; TR increases.
• EPD = 0 Perfectly inelastic demand
• EPD = ∞ Perfectly elastic demand

Forms of Demand
￼
￼
￼
• Individual Demand:
a) One type of good that a single consumer buys at
various prices at given time period
b) The demand for the output of an individual firm
(operating in perfectly or imperfectly competitive
market)
• Market Demand is the sum of demands of all
potential customers (market participants) for a specific
product over a specific period in a specific market
• Aggregate Demand is the total amount of goods
(products and services) demanded in the economy at
any given price at any point in time

Supply (S)
• Definition - Quantity of goods that firms are willing
to sell at various prices
• Quantity supplied – the amount of a good that firms
want to sell at a given price (holding contant other
factors that influence firms‘ supply decisions, such
as costs and government actions)
• Supply curve – the quantity supplied at each
possible price (holding constant other factors that
can influence it)

￼
