=== SLIDE 3 ===
Two written tests containing theory and computation. Both tests are obligatory for
all students!!
Students must have a minimum of 20 points from the written tests to go to the oral
exam. If a student does not earn the required minimum number of points, he or she may
take a retake test covering topics from the entire course, i.e., both Test 1 and Test 2.
Test duration: max. 45 min (usually 30 min). A sample test will be available at least 1
week before testing. In the case of cheating, a student immediately forfeits the privilege of
taking the test and earns ZERO points from the test. Such a test cannot be retaken!
A structure of the test:
I. Definition, explanation of terms, principles, variables....
II. Computation
III. Graphs
IV. Formulas
1st test (mid-term)
 Maximum 20 points
Week 8 during the seminar. Details will be specified later.
Content of the 1st test:
1. The subject and method of economics.
2. Theory of the Consumer: cardinal and ordinal approach; substitution in consumption (MRSC),
budget constraint, optimum.
3. Demand: demand curve, substitution and income effects. State intervention: price floor, price
ceiling.
4. Price elasticity of demand, consumer surplus.
5. Theory of the firm: transaction costs and the nature of the firm; goals of a firm; the production
function and costs: short run, long run; marginal rate of technical substitution (MRTS).
6. Revenues, demand.
2nd test (end-term)
 maximum 20 points
Week 13 during the seminar. Details will be specified later.
Content of the 2nd test:
7. Firm in perfect competition – optimum in the short and long run. Market equilibrium and market
price; benefits of exchange, changes in equilibrium.
8. Imperfect markets: price creation in an imperfect market, monopoly, monopolistic competition,
oligopoly.
9. Alternative goals of firms, principal-agent problem.
10. Specific features of the market of production factors. Agriculture (land) market.
11. Labor markets: production function and labor demand, market supply of labor, equilibrium of
the labor market.

3. Oral exam
A student goes to the oral exam if he/she has reached at least 20 points from tests. If
students do not meet this requirement, they receive a final grade of "4".
If students are not successful on the oral examination, they can receive a grade of 4+ on the oral
examination and retake it. In total, each student has two attempts to pass the oral exam (the
regular attempt and one "re-take" attempt). If a student cheats while preparing for an oral exam,
the student will receive a failing grade of “4.”
First expected date of oral exam for “early birds” (©): December 2026 (after the final test and its
review) and later.
Oral exam procedure at school: blind choice of topic, written preparation of approximately 15
min, oral examination, final grading (1-2-3-4 or 4+).
The current list of exam questions will be available in the InSIS documents.
